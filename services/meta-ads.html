What is Meta Ads (Facebook & IG)?

Meta Ads allow you to target users based on their deepest interests, behaviors, and demographics. Unlike Google Ads (which captures existing demand), Meta Ads allow you to *generate* demand by putting compelling offers in front of your exact target audience.
